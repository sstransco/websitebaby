About Us

Who We Are

Sigma Squared Transport Corporation is an asset-based carrier founded in 2025 and headquartered in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. We operate a growing fleet of late-model tractors across all 48 contiguous states, hauling dry van, refrigerated, and flatbed freight.

  • > Owner-Operated: Lean leadership, fast decisions, direct accountability on every load.
  • > Technology-First: Proprietary dispatch platform with AI-driven document processing, real-time GPS telemetry, and automated invoicing.
  • > Fully Compliant: ELD-equipped fleet, $1M auto liability, $1M cargo insurance, and real-time Samsara telemetry on every unit.
  • > Reliable Capacity: On-time delivery is the standard, not the exception. We run where freight needs to move.

We built our own technology because we believe the best carriers will be the ones that move faster, invoice cleaner, and communicate in real time.
